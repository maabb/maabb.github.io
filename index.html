Who Am I?

I’m Maabb Zakour.

I met programming years ago and never really put it down. What started as curiosity turned into building real systems people rely on every day — APIs, web platforms, and tools that need to stay solid under pressure.

Today I spend most of my time in the .NET world — crafting backends, connecting frontends when needed, and exploring how AI fits into practical products. When something breaks, I dig in. When something works, I still look for how to make it cleaner.
